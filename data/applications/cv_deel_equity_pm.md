## How I Work

- **I leave my ego at home**, and I love the problem, never my solution. I want the decision, not the last word; attachment ships the wrong thing beautifully.
- **On greenfield I buy information first**, and I see the whole value chain, not just the backlog. Cheap experiments make expensive decisions safe(r); defect-to-fix time is a product metric.

Nobody knows which solution the market actually needs. Not us, not the customer, not the highest-paid opinion in the room. So I treat each decision as a bet: continuous discovery and cheap prototypes move us toward the answer, pilot customers verify or buy failure information cheaply. Discovery is continuous, never a phase, and my discovery core is a product designer, the lead engineer and me. I negotiate outcomes, not output, and define success before we build: our intended R&S workflow lost to a measured alternative with exactly zero clicks. My working mode is AI-first, with my own multi-agent framework in production, so cutting manual operational overhead is daily practice rather than a theme to ramp on.
