I live in Limassol, and I have been on the other side of your product for years. Eight years of active trading, equities and crypto, and a backtesting environment I built myself at phoenix882.com because I wanted to test a strategy before putting capital behind it. So when your posting asks for a good understanding of financial markets or a strong willingness to learn, I am on the first half of that sentence.

What I would bring on top of being a user is a product practice built around evidence rather than opinion. Your posting names validating hypotheses through A/B testing and experimentation, and that is the part of the job I care most about. On the platform I ran at Rohde & Schwarz, I pushed to join the customer workshops until I was invited, and within one session it was clear that our users worked nothing like we had assumed. Instead of arguing about it, we shipped a second, code-heavy workflow next to the one we had planned and measured which of the two got used. Ours received exactly zero clicks. Learning that in a week was worth far more than a year of confident building. At EMIL I instrumented a claims flow so that churn per process step became the leading prioritisation metric, which is what told us which flows to cut rather than polish.

On scale and on consumer craft: I shipped a native iOS and Android product at CLINET for several distinct user groups, and got there by cutting an overambitious roadmap down to the most important ten percent. I founded a mobile payments company that was acquired and is operated by Sparkasse, and spent eight years as its CFO, so the commercial side of a product is not a foreign language to me either. Currently I own a two-sided marketplace end to end, from vision and multi-year roadmap through acquisition, activation and retention.

One thing about how I work with design, because your posting asks for UX sensibility. I do not hand flows to a designer and wait. My discovery core is always at least a product designer, the lead engineer and me, from the moment the problem is framed. Designers who are in the room that early stop producing decoration, and engineers who see the user problem early propose things nobody would have specified.

I would be glad to talk, and I can be in your office the same day.
